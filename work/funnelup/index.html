Workflow 1 of 2

Lead source follow-up

Every new lead is tagged by where they came from, so follow-up and reporting match the source.

  1. 01 · TriggerAI Studio form submitted
  2. 02 · CheckWhere did the lead come from?
  3. If Social media→ Tag: social-media
  4. If Website form→ Tag: website-form
  5. If Referral→ Tag: referral, then webhook to the referral flow
  6. If None→ End
  7. 03END
Workflow 2 of 2

Send voucher to referrer

When a referral comes in, the referrer is found in the CRM and gets their voucher by email.

  1. 01 · TriggerInbound webhookGets the referrer's email
  2. 02Find contact
  3. 03 · CheckContact found?Yes → send voucher. No → stop.
  4. 04Email: referral voucher
  5. 05END
